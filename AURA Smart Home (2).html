The AURA smart switch

We plan to offer the whole switch, not just a chip: a switch with the control chip already built in, so it works by hand and from your phone.

Designed with your privacy in mind. Technical details are still being developed and are not shared on this page.

Features of the AURA smart switch

What you get with the switch and its phone app.

Floor and room folders

Open a floor, pick a room and see every switch in it.

One button for every appliance

Bulbs, lights, fans, AC, heaters, coolers and more.

Fan speed and brightness

Slow down or speed up a fan. Dim or brighten a light.

Search or scroll

The home page lists every button. Type a name to find it.

Works by hand and by phone

The chip is built into the switch, so the switch still works as usual.

Clear names

Each button shows a name like Outside light, not just a board.

Security and privacy
